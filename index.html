About

A Finance Professional Focused on Value Creation

CA Anand Pravin Pande is a finance and strategic advisory professional with over 15 years of experience across accounting, valuation, insolvency, transaction advisory, business strategy and financial decision-making.

As a Partner at S P A R K & Co., Chartered Accountants, he works closely with businesses, promoters and other stakeholders on matters that extend beyond conventional accounting and compliance, with a strong focus on value creation, financial sustainability, risk assessment and strategic decision-making.

His work combines financial analysis, commercial understanding, regulatory perspective and structured risk assessment to provide solutions that are practical and decision-oriented.
